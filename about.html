About 3Gs & 3Bs Bakery

Welcome to 3Gs & 3Bs Bakery! We are a friendly local bakery in Kampala passionate about creating delicious, freshly baked treats every day. Our journey began with a simple love for baking quality bread, mouth-watering cakes, and golden pastries that bring families and friends together.

We believe that good baking starts with the best ingredients. From our fluffy birthday and wedding cakes to our tasty cookies and donuts, everything is prepared with care, hygiene, and attention to detail. Whether you need a quick sweet snack or a customized cake for your special occasion, we have something for everyone.

Our mission is to serve our community with warmth and joy. We take pride in reliable customer service, timely deliveries, and affordable prices for all our baked goods. Thank you for choosing 3Gs & 3Bs Bakery, where every single bite is freshly baked with love!
